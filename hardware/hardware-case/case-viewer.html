Hardware

  • 4 × M3 × 6 screws to hold the Uno on its posts. Use these: without them the Uno can slide around inside. Turn slowly and stop when snug.
  • 4 × M3 × 6–8 flat-head screws for the lid (optional). Without them the lid stays on by friction.
  • 2 × M4 or #8 screws for the keyhole ears (optional)
  • 1 small zip tie, up to 3 mm wide, for the cable
